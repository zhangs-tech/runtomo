# Tomo Asset Generation Prompts (Soft Pastel Kawaii Style)

This document provides ready-to-use prompts and art style formulas for generating Tomo character assets.

> **Visual Style Reference:** Japanese pastel kawaii anime mascot art (similar to San-X, Sanrio, and cozy Pokémon pastel illustrations). Features ultra-soft rounded baby proportions, delicate warm-brown lineart (no harsh black borders), soft pastel watercolor shading, warm airbrushed blush, and clean white backgrounds.

---

## 1. Master Style Formula & Core Rules

### Why the Previous Prompts Looked Stiff
- Prompts specifying `"thick clean dark outline"` or `"2D game sprite art"` cause generators to produce harsh black borders, corporate flash vector art, or rigid clipart.
- To achieve the cozy, adorable look of the reference:
  1. Replace black outlines with **delicate warm brown / sepia lineart**.
  2. Replace flat cel shading with **soft pastel watercolor and gentle airbrushed blush**.
  3. Emphasize **squishy baby-animal chibi proportions** (chubby cheeks, tiny rounded paws, marshmallow curves).
  4. Keep surfaces **completely smooth with ZERO fur texture or fluff**: Avoid fluffy collars, feathering, or hair strands. Characters share Rocky's clean, smooth plushie-like silhouette (similar to Sanrio or smooth Pokémon art).

### Master Formula Template

```text
Cute pastel kawaii anime illustration of [Subject Description], [Element Cue], [Fitness Accessory], [Pose & Expression], [Color Palette]. Soft squishy baby chibi proportions, smooth sleek body with zero fur texture, chubby cheeks with soft airbrushed rosy blush, large soulful glossy dark eyes with warm white reflections, sweet wholesome expression. Delicate warm brown lineart, soft pastel watercolor washes, gentle ambient shading. Clean Japanese mascot style. Isolated on pure solid white background, no fluff, no fur texture, no harsh black lines, no 3D rendering, no text.
```

### Universal Negative Prompt (Midjourney `--no` / Stable Diffusion)

```text
fluff, fur texture, fluffy ruff, hair strands, fuzzy outlines, feathering, harsh black outlines, thick black borders, vector flash art, corporate clipart, saturated neon colors, dark shadows, high contrast, 3D CGI render, photorealistic, complex background, floor shadows, text, watermark, signature
```

## 4. Generator Specific Tuning

### Midjourney (v6.1)
Append to prompt:
```text
--ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --style raw --s 180 --no black outlines, thick borders, dark shadows, realistic, 3d, vector art
```
*(Tip: `--s 180` softens the linework, and `--style raw` avoids Midjourney's default high-contrast cinematic lighting).*

### DALL-E 3 / ChatGPT
Paste directly as written, or prefix with:
```text
Please create this in a soft pastel Japanese kawaii anime illustration style with delicate warm brown lines (no harsh black outlines) and soft watercolor airbrushing: [Prompt]
```

### Stable Diffusion (SDXL / Animagine XL / Pony)
- **Positive Prompt Boosters:** `(soft pastel kawaii anime illustration:1.2), (warm brown lineart:1.1), soft watercolor, airbrushed blush, squishy chibi baby animal, fluffy, pure white background, masterpiece, best quality`
- **Negative Prompt:** `(thick black outline:1.3), (black borders:1.3), vector art, flash clipart, 3d, photorealistic, harsh shadows, dark lighting, saturated colors, lowres`
- **Sampler:** DPM++ 2M Karras or Euler a, 28–30 steps, CFG 6.0–7.0 (lower CFG keeps colors softer).
